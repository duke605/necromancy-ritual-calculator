## What the calculator does

The calculator plans Necromancy rituals. You choose a ritual, its focus item and where you perform it. The calculator then lists the items the rituals use and make, how long they take, and the experience they give.

Your choices are saved in your browser. They are still there the next time you open the site.

## Choose a ritual

Select the **Focus** button in **Results**, or the focus item in the middle of the ritual site. Then:

1. In **Ritual**, choose the ritual. The number after each name is the Necromancy level it needs.
2. In **Focus item**, choose the item you place on the focus. Some rituals accept more than one.
3. Select **Choose**.

The ritual's glyphs appear on the site, arranged around the focus.

## Choose a site

Use the **Site** list to choose where you perform the ritual.

| Site           | Effect                                                    |
| -------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| The Underworld | No change.                                                |
| Ungael         | Glyphs last 20% longer. Rituals give 20% less experience. |

## Set the number of rituals

The **Rituals** field in **Results** sets how many times you perform the ritual. Until you enter a number, it uses the golden ratio.

### Golden ratio

Each glyph wears out after a set number of rituals and has to be drawn again. The golden ratio is the smallest number of rituals after which every glyph wears out at the same time. At that count, you never redraw a glyph that still has uses left.

The field shimmers when the count is a multiple of the golden ratio. Use the arrows on either side of the field to move to the previous or next multiple.

## Start again

Each panel has a red reset button in its title bar, beside the arrow. It clears only that panel.

**Reset all**, beside the **Site** list, clears the ritual, site, glyphs, equipment and ritual count. It keeps your settings, inventory and prices.
