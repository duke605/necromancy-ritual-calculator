## Alteration glyphs

Alteration glyphs change how a ritual behaves. You place them in the spots the ritual's own glyphs leave free. The panel shows how many free spots are left.

| Glyph      | Effect                                           |
| ---------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| Multiply   | Makes more of everything the ritual makes.       |
| Speed      | Makes the ritual shorter, by up to 50% in total. |
| Protection | Has no effect on the results.                    |
| Attraction | Has no effect on the results.                    |

Every alteration glyph also raises soul attraction. Each one costs ink and ectoplasm to draw, and wears out faster than the ritual's own glyphs.

To remove one kind of glyph, select the **×** before its number. To remove all of them, use the reset button at the top of the panel.

The calculator saves the alteration glyphs for each ritual separately. They come back when you choose that ritual again.

## Equipment

Select a slot in the **Equipment** panel to choose what you wear in it. Only gear that changes rituals is listed.

| Gear                      | Effect                                                              |
| ------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Ritualist's outfit        | 1% more experience for each piece, and 1% more for the full set.    |
| Modified ritualist's mask | Counts as the outfit's mask.                                        |
| Alteration necklace       | Alteration glyphs are 20% stronger, including the one on your cape. |
| Underworld Grimoire       | 5%, 10% or 15% more necroplasm from tiers 2, 3 and 4.               |
| Ring of Kayazu            | 80% more soul attraction.                                           |
| Necromancy cape           | Applies one alteration glyph without drawing it. See below.         |

### Ritualist's outfit

To wear the whole outfit at once, open any of its slots and choose **Entire Ritualist's Outfit**. The **(modified)** option uses the modified mask instead.

### Necromancy cape

The Necromancy cape and the Necromancy master cape each hold one alteration glyph. That glyph works as if it were drawn on the site. It does not take a spot and does not cost ink or ectoplasm.

To set the glyph, open the **Back** slot, choose a cape, then choose a glyph in **Alteration glyph**. The number after each glyph is the Necromancy level it needs. In the game, you need that level without boosts to set the glyph.
